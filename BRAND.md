## Identity idea

The Matter icon is a compact material stack rather than a lettermark. It represents a source image being read as separate visual substances and recomposed into one object.

Its fixed geometry contains:

- a **frosted rounded-square tray**, the working surface
- an **information card**, representing source semantics and protected identity
- an **architecture / photographic card**, representing retained photography
- a **halftone card**, representing print transformation and graphic reduction
- a **translucent cyan tape strip**, representing constructed collage
- a **lavender circular marker**, providing the secondary material accent

The depth order is structural: the halftone card remains in front of the architecture card everywhere they overlap.

## Asset rules

- Keep light and dark variants on identical geometry.
- Preserve the corrected card depth order.
- Do not turn the icon into an M monogram.
- Do not introduce warm ivory / beige brand fields.
- Do not replace the collage with a generic sparkle, wand, gradient orb, or AI-star symbol.
- Keep blue as the dominant chromatic anchor, cyan as a translucent construction material, and lavender as a small secondary accent.
- Small-size exports may simplify texture, but must preserve the layered-card silhouette and front/back relationship.
